# Operating model

The accountable root owns the intended outcome and next useful action. The
[shared engineering questions](00-doctrine.md#shared-questions) apply to roots,
writers and reviewers. Command and agent names help find capabilities; they do
not prescribe a roster or give each agent a permanent stage of the lifecycle.

## Start from the current claim

Inspect accepted intent, the existing issue or candidate, actual source,
material findings and accessible evidence. Identify the earliest useful missing
judgment. A candidate with an implementation defect needs a repair; a candidate
with a mistaken premise needs the premise challenged. Neither must replay all
historical flow artifacts before useful work can continue.

GitHub owns live issue, PR, review, check and merge facts. Git owns versioned
source, specifications and decisions. Native workers retain unpublished work,
processes and worktrees. Preserve important history and reasons for changes;
do not maintain a second live-state or writer-heartbeat database in source.

## Work and delegation

The root may investigate, implement, test, review or integrate directly within
its assignment. Delegate when another context, instrument, oracle, perspective
or available capacity improves the result. A bounded child receives the actual
claim, source and evidence it needs, the useful question, permitted effects and
the owner who continues after its return. See [the agent contract](50-agent-contract.md).

Keep one mutation writer per candidate and coordinate actual overlapping
effects. Read-only investigation and review need no reservation. A new reader
does not take over the writer's branch, and a returning child does not complete
the parent's goal. Join findings and route accepted repairs through the writer.

Skills and tools supply scoped observations and operations. Check what they
actually executed, their inputs and limitations. A deterministic tool can still
inspect the wrong subject or have a weak oracle; an exit code alone does not
establish the intended outcome.

## Keep useful context

Use an existing warm session for successive questions, repairs and final
judgment when it helps. If the harness can keep candidate subjects, worktrees
and pending operations distinct, a session may handle several PRs serially or
concurrently. Independent objectives may also use parallel sessions. A short,
bounded session remains valid; no duration or number of agents is prescribed.

Several Draft alternatives may be reviewed cheaply. Select the candidate
intended to land for each objective before its expensive qualification. This
does not impose a global one-PR limit on independent objectives.

For an ordinary CI wait, retain the accountable lead and useful context when
the harness supports it. Follow the actual operation through event-driven or
bounded checks, and do independent work when useful. Do not use a tight polling
worker or force a new owner merely because a normal wait began. A real
interruption, session limit or longer wait needs a recoverable handoff with the
native operation identity, wake condition and continuing owner.

## Choose the next useful action

Explain what is missing and why the next action can resolve it. A routing
suggestion names work and evidence before it names an agent. For example, a
weak test oracle needs a discriminating counterexample, not an automatic call
to every test-related role. If a supposedly missing repair has already landed,
inspect and compose it instead of creating a competing fix or waiting on its
old owner report.

Research derivable questions and use reasonable assumptions within the accepted
scope. Ask for a decision at the actual boundary when intent or authority is
missing. An instrument failure, absent capability, exhausted capacity and an
unresolved product choice are different limitations; keep independent useful
work moving and do not misreport any of them as a pass.

## Complete the selected outcome

The current Build/Review/Gate/Deploy commands and
[late-qualification reference](../../docs/reference/late-qualification.md)
separate publication, substantive review, explicit qualification, final
judgment and protected merge. Ordinary publication and Ready share work;
they do not request qualification. The installed consumer adapter supplies the
actual request operation; an absent adapter is a named implementation gap.

After complete qualification, the same authorized lead can make the separate
fresh judgment over findings, product intent, current base and evidence
applicability, then perform the protected merge and landed readback. Green CI
does not decide this for the lead. Use protected merge without routine
auto-merge or a queue.
Release and installed delivery keep their own authorization and evidence.

Report the claim actually completed, useful original evidence, remaining
limitation and next action. Opening a PR, finishing a child task or merging a
leaf does not complete a larger outcome.
